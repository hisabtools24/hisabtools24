What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount generally paid every month towards repayment of a loan. An EMI usually consists of both principal and interest.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI depends mainly on the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI but may increase the total interest paid over the loan period.

Where can you use this calculator?

You can use this calculator for estimating EMI for personal loans, home loans, car loans and other loans.
