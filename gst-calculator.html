What is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax applied to the supply of many goods and services in India.

How is GST calculated?

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

CGST and SGST

For an intra-state transaction, GST may be divided into CGST and SGST. For example, an 18% GST rate may be divided into 9% CGST and 9% SGST.
