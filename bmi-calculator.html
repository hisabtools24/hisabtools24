What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a simple measurement calculated using a person's weight and height.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (metres)

General BMI Categories for Adults

Below 18.5: Underweight

18.5 – 24.9: Healthy weight

25 – 29.9: Overweight

30 or above: Obesity

Important Note

BMI is only a general screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or overall health. It may not be appropriate for every person. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for individual health advice.
