What is a Discount?

A discount is a reduction from the original price of a product or service. Discounts are commonly shown as a percentage.

How to Calculate Discount?

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

If a product costs ₹1,000 and has a 20% discount, the discount amount is ₹200 and the final price is ₹800.

Important Note

This calculator provides estimates for general informational purposes. Taxes, delivery charges and other fees may affect the final amount paid.
