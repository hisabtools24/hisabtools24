What is Compound Interest?

Compound Interest is interest calculated on the original principal amount plus the interest accumulated over previous periods.

Compound Interest Formula

A = P × (1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ

Here, P is the principal amount, r is the annual interest rate, n is the number of times interest is compounded per year, and t is the time in years.

Example

If ₹10,000 is invested at an annual rate of 8% for 5 years, the final amount depends on how frequently the interest is compounded.
