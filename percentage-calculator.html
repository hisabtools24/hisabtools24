How to Calculate Percentage?

Percentage means a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The basic percentage formula is:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

Example

If you want to know what percentage 25 is of 200:

(25 ÷ 200) × 100 = 12.5%

Percentage Increase and Decrease

Percentage calculations are commonly used for marks, discounts, salary changes, price changes, business calculations and many other everyday situations.
