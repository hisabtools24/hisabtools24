What is a SIP?

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It allows an investor to invest a fixed amount regularly, usually every month, in a mutual fund or other investment product.

How does SIP work?

In a SIP, you invest a fixed amount at regular intervals. The investment can benefit from compounding over a long period. Actual returns may vary depending on market performance.

Example

If you invest ₹5,000 every month for 10 years and assume an annual return of 12%, the calculator will estimate your total investment, estimated returns and final value.

Important Note

SIP calculator results are estimates only. Actual mutual fund returns are not guaranteed and can be higher or lower depending on market conditions.
