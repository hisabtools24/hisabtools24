What is Simple Interest?

Simple Interest is interest calculated only on the original principal amount for a specific period of time.

Simple Interest Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100

Example

If you invest ₹10,000 at 5% annual interest for 2 years, the simple interest will be ₹1,000 and the final amount will be ₹11,000.

Important Note

This calculator provides mathematical estimates only. Actual interest may vary depending on the terms and conditions of your bank, lender or investment.
